# Neon Tetris Completed!

The game implementation is now fully complete! We have built a premium, modern Tetris clone featuring a neon glow aesthetic and glassmorphism UI. 

## Features Delivered
- **Classic Tetris Gameplay**: Full 10x20 board with 7 distinct and vibrant Tetrominoes.
- **Advanced Mechanics**: 
  - **Ghost Piece**: Shows exactly where your piece will land.
  - **Hold Piece (`C`)**: Swap your current piece to save it for the perfect moment.
  - **Next Piece Preview**: Always know what's coming next.
  - **Hard Drop (`Space`)**: Instantly drop pieces for faster gameplay and bonus points.
- **Scoring & Progression**: Clearing 1, 2, 3, or 4 lines awards points using the classic multiplier system. Every 10 lines clears increases your level, making the pieces fall faster!
- **Premium Aesthetics**: Deep space gradient background, animated glowing titles, translucent glass panels, and satisfying CSS micro-animations (e.g., canvas shake on game over, brightness flash on level up).

## How to Play
A local HTTP server has been started for you. 
**Open your browser to**: [http://localhost:8080](http://localhost:8080)

1. Click **PLAY** to start.
2. Use **Arrow Keys** to move (`Left/Right`) and rotate (`Up`). Soft drop with `Down`.
3. Use **Space** for a Hard Drop.
4. Press **C** to Hold a piece.
5. Press **P** to Pause the game.
