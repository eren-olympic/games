HITORI

A Zen Logic Puzzle

How to Play

  • No Duplicates: Unshaded rows and columns cannot have repeating numbers.
  • No Touching: Shaded squares cannot share an edge (no orthogonal adjacency).
  • Connected Path: All unshaded squares must connect to each other in a continuous group.

Controls:

  • Left Click: Shade / Unshade a cell.
  • Right Click: Circle a cell to mark it as safe.
